nabla: already CRAN 0.7.1, published 2026-02-10. Baseline full test suite passes.
Source: https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/nabla/index.html
Useful role: pure-R low-dimensional higher derivatives and pedagogical nested duals.
Limitations: no dual_vector arithmetic/matrix algebra/base distribution dispatch; D evaluates p^k original functions at order k. Existing README overclaims arbitrary R code.
Confirmed/fixed under nabla 0.7.2 working tree:
- powers evaluated at zero returned NaN above polynomial degree;
- .is_zero() used is.numeric before dual class, dropping nested tangent curvature;
- cummax/cummin scalar derivatives incorrectly zero, with old tests encoding incorrect answer.

femtograd: not present in CRAN available.packages index. Baseline suite running.
